# OWDE 0.6.0-alpha.1

## Canonical Optical and Physical Primitives

This release establishes canonical geometry for five highly visible
Operational Worlds primitives.

## OW09 Lens


• Perfectly circular from above.
• Rotationally symmetric.
• Low surface-tension water profile.
• Centered apex.
• No pedestal or separate mounting platform.
• Clear transmissive material.

## OW10 Mirror


• Square reflective surface.
• Thin off-white structural bezel.
• Mirror surface elevated above the bezel.
• Chrome reflective material assigned only to the central plate.

## OW11 Light Source


• Low frosted bulb profile.
• Short cylindrical neck.
• Reduced overall height.
• Smooth rounded crown.
• Warm emissive translucent material.

## OW12 Weight


• Solid cubic mass.
• Rotated 45 degrees around the vertical axis.
• Chamfered vertical edges.
• Brushed-metal material.
• Directly supported by the invariantal detector dome.
• Shallow off-white mounting recess.
• Piano-black finish assigned only to the dome.
• Reduced scale relative to Lens and Light Source.

## Preserved systems


• Invariant rectangular substrate.
• All fifteen primitive definitions.
• Primitive Gallery.
• Primitive Inspector.
• Documentation Showcase.
• Individual showcase renders.
• Flat-top hexagon.
• Manufacturing package exporter.
